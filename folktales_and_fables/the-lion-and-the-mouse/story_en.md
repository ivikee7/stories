# The Lion and the Mouse (शेर और नन्हा चूहा)

## Scene 1: The Sleeping Monarch
**Visual Cue**: Cinematic 3D animation, a massive majestic Bengal lion with golden mane sleeping peacefully under the shade of an ancient giant banyan tree in a lush green jungle, warm morning sunlight, pure pictorial illustration without text.

**Narrator**: In the heart of a vast and ancient jungle, sheltered beneath the cool shade of a giant banyan tree, the mighty King Lion was sleeping peacefully. Golden afternoon sunlight filtered through the leaves, and the forest was serene.

---

## Scene 2: The Playful Intruder
**Visual Cue**: Close-up 3D animation of a tiny adorable brown field mouse with large shiny black eyes, playfully scampering along the massive hairy paw of the sleeping lion, colorful forest flowers nearby.

**Narrator**: Just then, from a tiny burrow nearby, an energetic and curious little mouse named Cheeku popped out. Playfully scampering through the moss, he accidentally climbed right onto the lion's massive paw and slid down his nose.

---

## Scene 3: The Lion's Wrath
**Visual Cue**: Dramatic wide shot, the giant muscular lion wakes up with glowing furious amber eyes, slamming his massive clawed paw down, trapping the tiny terrified mouse beneath his claws.

**Narrator**: The lion awakened with a start. Enraged that his royal slumber had been interrupted, the beast let out a thunderous growl. Raising his enormous clawed paw, he pinned tiny Cheeku firmly to the earth.

---

## Scene 4: A Tiny Plea
**Visual Cue**: Emotional close-up 3D animation, tiny mouse folding its little paws in desperate plea, tears glistening in its big eyes, looking up fearfully at the towering lion's open jaws.

**Narrator**: Cheeku trembled in sheer terror. Folding his tiny paws, he pleaded desperately: 'O mighty King of the Jungle! Please forgive me. It was an accident! If you spare my life today, I will never forget your mercy, and one day, I may even help you in return!'

---

## Scene 5: The King's Laughter
**Visual Cue**: Wide angle shot, the giant lion throwing his head back roaring in hearty amused laughter, looking down with a smirk at the tiny mouse, lifting his paw to let him go.

**Narrator**: Hearing a tiny creature claim he could help the King of the Jungle, the lion threw his head back and roared with laughter. 'Ha ha ha! A speck of dust like you helping me? Your audacity amuses me, little one. Run along before I change my mind!'

---

## Scene 6: The Hunter's Trap
**Visual Cue**: Cinematic dramatic lighting, twilight forest, the massive lion hopelessly entangled and hanging inside a thick heavy rope hunter's net tied to jungle trees, roaring in distress.

**Narrator**: A few nights later, while stalking the moonlit woods, the great lion stepped right into a trap laid by ruthless hunters. A thick rope net snared him, hauling him against the banyan trunk. The more he struggled, the tighter the ropes bound him, until he roared in agony.

---

## Scene 7: A Debt Repaid
**Visual Cue**: Dynamic close-up 3D shot, Cheeku the brave mouse rapidly nibbling through the thick fibrous hunter's ropes with his sharp tiny teeth, fibers tearing apart, lion watching in awe.

**Narrator**: Recognizing the cry of distress, Cheeku sprinted through the darkness. Seeing his benefactor trapped, the mouse wasted no time. With his sharp, relentless teeth, he began gnawing through the heavy hemp ropes. One by one, the thick strands snapped.

---

## Scene 8: Friends Forever
**Visual Cue**: Warm golden sunrise over the jungle, the magnificent freed lion gently nudging the proud little mouse sitting on his paw, smiling warmly together as loyal best friends, vibrant colorful foliage.

**Narrator**: The ropes gave way, and the great lion tumbled safely to freedom. Humbled and deeply moved, the king lowered his mighty head and offered his paw to the little hero. 'Cheeku,' the lion whispered, 'you have taught me that no creature is too small to make a difference.' From that day on, the lion and the mouse walked the forest as inseparable friends.
