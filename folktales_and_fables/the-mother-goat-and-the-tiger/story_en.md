# The Mother Goat and the Tiger (बुद्धिमान बकरी और बाघ)

## Characters:
- **Gauri:** A brave, clever, and fiercely protective mother goat.
- **Munna:** Gauri’s innocent, playful little baby goat.
- **Shera:** A proud, ferocious, and slightly gullible Bengal tiger.

---

### Scene 1: The Safe Meadow
At the edge of the great Sundarban forest, in a peaceful sunlit meadow, lived a remarkably clever mother goat named Gauri, along with her playful baby, Munna. Every morning, warm golden sunlight bathed the lush grass as dew drops sparkled like diamonds. Munna hopped joyfully around while Gauri grazed peacefully, keeping a watchful eye. She always reminded him, "My little child, never wander deep into the dark forest. Ferocious predators lurk behind the shadows."

### Scene 2: The Lost Kid
One afternoon, while Gauri was drinking clear water from a nearby forest stream, Munna spotted a dazzling, iridescent blue butterfly. Entranced by its fluttering wings, he chased it across the blooming wildflowers. Deeper and deeper into the dark, shadowy woods he went. When the butterfly suddenly fluttered high into the leafy canopy and disappeared, Munna looked around and gasped. Towering trees blocked the sky in eerie silence. Trembling in panic, tears filled his eyes as he cried: "Maaa... Oh Maaa! Where are you?"

### Scene 3: The Tiger's Ambush
Munna’s frightened bleat echoed through the dense jungle. The sound reached the sharp ears of Shera, the fiercest and most feared tiger in the entire wilderness. Shera had been prowling hungry since sunrise. Parting the thick bushes, his fiery amber eyes locked onto the plump, trembling white kid. Licking his lips in wicked anticipation, the giant predator stepped forward and blocked the trail.
Shera rumbled in a deep, menacing roar: "Well, well! What a delightful surprise. A tender, juicy snack has walked straight into my dining hall. Say your last prayers, little one!"

### Scene 4: A Mother's Courage
Just as Shera crouched low, preparing to pounce, branches snapped violently. Gauri burst through the dense undergrowth! Any ordinary creature would have collapsed in sheer terror at the sight of the giant tiger. But Gauri knew that even a moment of fear would spell death for her beloved child. Gathering every ounce of motherly love and courage, she leaped boldly between Shera and Munna. She puffed out her chest, lowered her sharp horns, and stared fearlessly into the tiger's ferocious eyes.

### Scene 5: The Clever Bluff
Shera froze in astonishment, taken aback by the goat's sudden defiance.
Growling fiercely, Shera snorted: "Have you lost your mind, goat? I am Shera, the undisputed king of this jungle! Step aside immediately before I tear you both apart!"
Gauri did not flinch an inch. Instead, she laughed loudly and boomed with fearless authority: "Eat me? Hahaha! You foolish beast, you should be bowing down in gratitude that I am sparing your miserable life today! Have you not heard of the legendary Iron-Horned Beast of the high peaks? I have already feasted on three giant tigers for breakfast this morning, and I was just looking for a fourth tiger to complete my lunch!"

### Scene 6: The Tiger's Doubt
Shera was completely stunned. In his entire life, no animal had ever stood up to him, let alone claimed to hunt and eat tigers!
Noticing the doubt in the predator's eyes, Gauri instantly pressed her advantage. She stomped her front hoof hard against the stony earth, kicking up a dramatic cloud of dust, and leveled her horns directly at his throat.
Gauri barked aggressively: "Why do you think my baby was crying? He was crying because he is hungry for fresh tiger meat! Step closer, Shera, let us see if your striped hide can withstand my horns!"

### Scene 7: The Escape
Gauri's supreme confidence and bloodcurdling bluff shattered Shera's nerve. The proud predator became convinced that this was no ordinary goat, but a mythical horned monster capable of tearing tigers to shreds.
Without waiting a single second longer, sheer terror gripped the great beast. With a cowardly whimper, Shera turned on his heels and bolted wildly into the dark jungle, his heavy paws scattering dry leaves as he vanished into the distant trees.

### Scene 8: Happy Reunion
As the sound of the fleeing tiger faded into silence, Gauri exhaled a long, trembling breath of relief. She turned tenderly to Munna, nuzzled his forehead, and licked away his tears. Munna buried his face against his brave mother's chest. Guiding him swiftly out of the shadows, they returned safely to the sunlit meadows. That day, little Munna learned an unforgettable lesson: no matter how terrifying the danger, courage, calm wit, and presence of mind can triumph over the greatest physical force.

**Moral of the Story:** Presence of mind, unwavering courage, and clever wit can defeat even the greatest brute strength.
